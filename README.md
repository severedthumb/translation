# Notes on translations of E. H. Gombrich
## What is this?
I'm learning German and Spanish.

To improve my reading skills in both languages, I am reading the English, German, and Spanish translations of Austrian/English historian E. H. Gombrich's "A Little History of the World" (or _Eine kurze Weltgeschichte für junge Leser_ — the German is the original).

I created this simple webpage to take notes as I work through the text.

The page compares the three texts side-by-side, one paragraph at a time, with room for my own notes.

I try to do about a paragraph each day. At that rate, this will probably take me about a year and a half to complete.

More features will be added in the future. For example, the ability to navigate from chapter to chapter.
